# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome.

- **Bugs:** open an issue with what you did, what you expected and what happened, plus the build number.
- **Pages that break:** include the address. If turning off ad blocking in Settings fixes it, say so; it's
  probably a filter-list rule.
- **Pull requests:** keep them focused on one change. Privacy defaults stay on: a change shouldn't make Airlock
  share more by default than it does today.
- **License:** by contributing you agree your work is released under the GNU GPL v3 or later, like the rest of Airlock.
